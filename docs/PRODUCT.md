# Product

Electric London is a private, mobile-first companion for Diego and María's London trip, 6–13 August 2026. A trip is explored as eight editorial chapters rather than consulted as a timetable.

The first slice proves three spaces: Journey for immersive day covers and strategic itineraries, Saved for flexible place ideas, and Trip for logistics. The plan distinguishes confirmed **anchors**, flexible **intentions**, and contextual **nearby options**. Supabase, editing and collaboration remain outside this phase.

Success means the selected day can be understood quickly, fixed plans stay visible without making the day rigid, and every spatial transition preserves the user's context.

Iteration 02 turns the approved Electric London direction into a system: Spanish-first content, recognizable media, responsive cover staging and one real bridge from Guardados to Días. It deliberately stops before persistence or full editing.
